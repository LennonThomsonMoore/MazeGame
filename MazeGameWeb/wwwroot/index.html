Hide and Seek Maze

A two-player maze game. One player is the Hider, the other the Seeker. Take turns moving through the maze one square at a time. The seeker wins on capture; the hider wins by surviving 100 turns. Positions are hidden except on reveal turns every 5 complete turns.
